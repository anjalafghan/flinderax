# Turn joy's ledger into a life plan

Joy already has most of the features the leading apps charge for. What it lacks is the data layer underneath them. The highest-value next step is not another planning screen but a reliable pipeline: clean merchants, detect transfers and recurring payments, ingest email alerts and mutual fund statements. Every existing feature (safe-to-spend, bills, the tax panel, net worth) becomes more accurate once that pipeline exists. In India, the regulated "proper" route, Account Aggregator, is closed to an individual developer. Bank SMS, emailed alerts and statements, netbanking exports, CAMS/KFintech CAS PDFs and the AIS tax JSON together cover almost everything an HDFC/Kotak customer with cards, SIPs, RDs and FDs owns, including joint accounts that AA excludes. On top of that pipeline, the best returns come from India-specific planning that global apps cannot offer: a failed-UPI refund tracker tied to RBI deadlines, interest-free-window card advice, LTCG harvesting per spouse, a regime picker, and goal and retirement maths with Indian defaults (10% education and medical inflation, a 3-3.5% safe withdrawal rate rather than 4%). For couples, research points to shared goals and transparent fairness, not merged accounts, as what protects relationships. Joy's date jar and settle-up already fit that model and need visibility controls, income-proportional splits and family-obligation tags. LLMs belong only at the edges: code computes every number, and the model labels merchants or narrates a precomputed month.

## Leading apps agree on tracking and compete on planning, partners and AI

By 2026 automatic categorisation, recurring and subscription detection, budgets, net worth and bill reminders are table stakes across every major app ([Copilot help](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/11157550-quick-start-guide); [Penny Hoarder](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/budgeting/best-budgeting-apps/)). The differentiators sit above that layer. **Copilot deliberately keeps recurring bills out of its "left to spend" figure so that rent doesn't read as overspending** ([Copilot](https://www.copilot.money/compare/rocket-money)). That is the same idea as joy's safe-to-spend-until-payday, which confirms joy's core metric matches a best-in-class pattern. Origin is the clearest "planning, not tracking" product. It bundles an AI advisor that answers personal questions, forecasting and scenarios, a Financial Health Score with prioritised actions, tax and estate suggestions, billing-cycle-aware credit tips such as lowering utilisation before the statement closes, and a free partner invite ([Origin](https://useorigin.com/)). Monarch includes a partner seat with separate logins over a shared budget ([Penny Hoarder](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/budgeting/best-budgeting-apps-couples/?aff_id=401)). AI tiers around $19.99/month are becoming the category's premium baseline ([pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/personal-finance/news/best-budgeting-apps-for-march-2026-8e3908eb-7888-442c-8875-08e5c04a4036)). Among self-hosted tools, Actual Budget competes on rules for payee renaming and category assignment plus schedules for recurring items ([elfhosted](https://store.elfhosted.com/product/actual-budget/)). Lunch Money's recurring items take flexible schedules and auto-match new transactions ([Lunch Money](https://lunchmoney.app/features/recurring-expenses/)).

India has split into two data pipelines. **CRED Money (July 2024) uses Account Aggregator to unify balances across banks and UPI IDs, analyse spend by merchant, and remind users about SIPs, EMIs, rent and premiums** ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/kunal-shah-led-cred-unveils-new-offering-to-help-customers-manage-bank-accounts-track-expenses); [goodreturns](https://www.goodreturns.in/classroom/cred-money-launched-an-all-in-one-pocket-to-manage-your-financial-transactions-how-to-use-1360011.html)). INDmoney adds a multi-card tracker with due-date reminders and goal planning that maps investments to goals ([INDmoney](https://www.indmoney.com/features/track-credit-card-bills); [equitiesindia](https://equitiesindia.com/tools-directory/indmoney-review)). Kuvera, which CRED acquired in 2024, is valued for goal planning and its nudge to harvest up to ₹1.25 lakh of LTCG tax-free each year ([equitiesindia](https://equitiesindia.com/tools-directory/kuvera-review)). The other pipeline is on-device SMS parsing: Walnut, now axio, and a 2025-26 wave of "100% local", no-login indie trackers such as MandateVault, which follows SIP mandates ([hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/mandatevault)). Neobanks are retreating. **Fi wound down its banking services in March 2026 after its Federal Bank partnership ended, despite about $169M raised and 3.5M+ customers** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/india-neobank-fi-winds-down-banking-services-on-its-platform)). Couples-first apps have fared worse. Zeta reportedly closed in May 2025 (per a competitor's blog, [pocketclear](https://pocketclear.app/blog/zeta-app-alternative-couples.html)), and Honeydue survives as a free, basic, ad-funded app with transaction chat but little forward planning ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/finance/learn/honeydue-app-review)). No Indian app in the research had couples-specific features.

Three conclusions follow. Joy is already competitive on the differentiators global apps gate behind premium tiers: forecasting, scenarios, "can I afford it". Its gaps are a health score with ranked actions, investment-to-goal mapping, and grounded AI Q&A. And its date jar, a sinking fund wrapped around shared experiences, has no equivalent anywhere in the market reviewed. Shared finance survives commercially as a feature inside general apps, which is exactly how joy is built. One caveat: much of the 2026 review material is affiliate or competitor content, and "most valued feature" is inferred from what vendors gate behind paywalls, not from measured usage.

## Account Aggregator is closed to individuals; email, SMS and CAS cover the gap

The AA network is mature: 17 AAs, about 176 FIPs, about 1,020 FIUs, and Sahamati recognised as its self-regulatory organisation on 5 June 2026. But **a data consumer (FIU) must be regulated by RBI, SEBI, IRDAI or PFRDA**. Technology providers such as Setu serve licensed FIUs and do not lend their licence. A first-year FIU build is estimated at ₹5-25 lakh over 5-10 months ([ecorpit](https://ecorpit.com/account-aggregator-integration-fintech-builders-2026/); [Setu docs](https://docs.setu.co/data/account-aggregator/licenses-and-go-live/go-live)). Even a licensed joy would find gaps where it matters most for this household: **AA excludes joint accounts, and only about 40% of banks expose FDs and RDs** ([ecorpit](https://ecorpit.com/account-aggregator-integration-fintech-builders-2026/)). AA is therefore a schema reference, not a data source. Shaping joy's ledger tables around the ReBIT deposit, term-deposit and mutual-fund schemas keeps a future pivot cheap.

The individual-accessible sources complement each other. **SMS is the fastest source but has gaps: HDFC stopped SMS alerts for UPI debits up to ₹100 and credits up to ₹500 in June 2024, while email alerts continue for every transaction** ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/industry/banks/story/hdfc-bank-to-stop-sms-alerts-for-upi-payments-up-to-rs-100-email-notifications-to-continue-431258-2024-05-29)). That makes email ingest the missing piece of joy's existing SMS feed. Google Play restricts READ_SMS to default handlers plus reviewed exceptions, which in practice are often denied ([Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10208820?hl=en); [Bluecoins](https://bluecoinsapp.com/?p=9286)). A sideloaded forwarder posting to joy's endpoint avoids that policy entirely. For sender filtering, TRAI's -S/-T/-P/-G header suffixes, enforced across telcos by July 2025, let joy accept only service and transactional messages and reject promotional lookalikes ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/07/223-telcos-suffixes-commercial-messages-users/)).

For bulk history, **HDFC netbanking exports PDF, Excel, Text, Delimited and MSMoney formats, so joy should parse the XLS or Delimited file rather than the PDF** ([HDFC FAQ](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/need-help/net-banking-faqs)). Emailed statements, card statements and CAS PDFs can arrive automatically through the Gmail API. Its restricted read scope caps unverified apps at 100 users, which is ample for a couple running in "Testing" mode ([Google Cloud](https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13463817)). IMAP with an app password is the alternative that needs no OAuth at all. For mutual funds, **the MIT-licensed `casparser` parses CAMS/KFintech and NSDL/CDSL CAS PDFs into JSON, including a Schedule 112A capital-gains report**. It rejects re-printed PDFs and does not support MF Central's template ([casparser](https://github.com/codereverser/casparser)). That makes a small Python sidecar the cheapest route to SIP holdings. For the annual tax view, AIS downloads as JSON and now holds interest, dividends and securities transactions, while 26AS shows only TDS/TCS ([Quicko](https://learn.quicko.com/ais-annual-information-statement)). Each credit bureau must provide one free full report a year ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/provide-free-annual-credit-report-individuals-rbi.html)).

| Source | What it gives joy | Freshness | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| SMS forwarder (existing) | Debits, credits, card spends, some balances | Seconds | Keep; misses small HDFC UPI |
| Email alerts + statements (Gmail Testing / IMAP) | All UPI alerts, card statements, CAS | Minutes to monthly | **Add next** |
| Netbanking XLS/Delimited (HDFC, Kotak) | Full history incl. joint accounts | Manual | Extend CSV import |
| CAMS/KFin CAS + casparser | All MF folios, SIP transactions, cost basis | On request or monthly | Add |
| AIS JSON | Tax-reported interest, dividends, TDS | Annual | Add to tax panel |
| EPFO passbook, credit report PDFs | EPF balance; open cards and loans | Manual | Optional |
| Account Aggregator | Everything above, near real time | Real time | Not feasible |

Several details remain unverified and must be checked against real inboxes: Kotak's export formats and e-statement passwords, exact HDFC and Kotak SMS and email templates, and the conflicting HDFC statement-password rules.

## Analytics start with clean merchants and transfer detection

Every downstream insight depends on parsing Indian narrations correctly, and they are messy. NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, NACH and UPI each carry different tokens. HDFC uses slashes where ICICI uses hyphens. CSV exports often truncate narrations to 50-100 characters, and a core-banking upgrade can silently break a parser, so match rates need monitoring ([Terra Insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/bank-statement-narration-patterns-india)). The VPA suffix identifies the payment app (@ybl and @axl for PhonePe, @ok* for Google Pay), not the merchant ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UPI_Apps)). The merchant key is the local part, as in `swiggy.stores@axl`. Plaid's industrial answer is a fine-tuned transformer foundation model, and its December 2025 upgrade claims up to 20% better subcategory accuracy ([Plaid](https://plaid.com/blog/ai-enhanced-transaction-categorization/)). For a single user, though, LLMs are a weak backbone: **zero-shot GPT-4o reached only 60.4% accuracy on terse bank descriptions, against about 73% for a fine-tuned model** ([arXiv 2508.05425](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.05425)). One household has a few hundred distinct merchants, so per-bank regex parsers, a seeded merchant alias table with fuzzy matching, and rules learned from every user correction will beat any generic model. An LLM should only suggest categories for the leftover unknown merchants, and joy should cache each answer. Just as important is marking self-transfers and card-bill payments as transfers and de-duplicating SMS/email/CSV copies by reference ID. Without that, savings rate and spend totals double count.

Recurring detection is a solved heuristic problem. Group by merchant, test the gaps between payments against canonical periods within a tolerance, and check that amounts are stable. One open-source detector uses at least two occurrences, a 35% default amount tolerance and a 5-day grace period, plus a known-subscription list that matches on a single charge ([subscription-detector](https://gigurra.github.io/subscription-detector/algorithm)). India adds explicit signals (NACH, UPI AutoPay and card standing-instruction narrations) that mark recurrence immediately, including SIP mandates. The output feeds joy's bills and commitments directly. It also adds price-hike alerts, late or missed bills, yearly renewals, trial conversions after a ₹1-2 verification charge, and salary detection for income dates.

Anomaly detection should use robust statistics, not ML. Median/MAD z-scores per merchant and category, duplicate-charge windows and fee keyword rules are explainable for one user's data. The standout India-specific feature is a failed-transaction tracker. **RBI's TAT framework requires failed UPI P2P debits to be reversed by T+1 and merchant UPI debits by T+5, with ₹100 per day compensation after that** ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11693)). Joy can open a case when it sees a failure SMS, match the reversal credit, and show days overdue and the escalation path. Secondary sources disagree on whether those are calendar or working days and whether payment is automatic, so the copy should hedge. A yearly "you paid ₹X in charges, GST and interest" card is another cheap win.

Joy's safe-to-spend can grow into a probabilistic cash-flow forecast. The deterministic calendar it already models (bills, card due dates, goal transfers, salary) stays. On top of it, joy bootstraps discretionary daily spend from history to produce P10/P50/P90 balance paths. **A 2025 banking cash-flow study found residual bootstrap came closer to nominal 80% coverage with narrower intervals than quantile regression** ([Barekeng](https://doaj.org/article/2a23d2ba21ff47288f8c3e459acebe21)). A low-balance warning fires when P10 crosses a threshold before payday. A nightly backtest that records forecast accuracy earns trust. Indian seasonality (Diwali, school fees, advance-tax dates, insurance renewals) is better captured as user-confirmed annual events than learned from one or two years of data.

The health metrics are mostly SQL views: savings rate (SIPs, RDs and PPF count as saving), income coefficient of variation, months of liquid cover, category trend slopes, and lifestyle creep, meaning discretionary spend growing faster than income after a raise. For the emergency buffer, **the JPMorgan Chase Institute found about 6.2 weeks of take-home pay covers a simultaneous income dip and expense spike, and 65% of families lacked it** ([JPMC Institute](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/institute/all-topics/household-financial-health/report-weathering-volatility-2-a-monthly-stress-test-to-guide-saving)). That gives joy a reachable first milestone before the conventional 3-6 months, though the data is American.

On credit cards, Indian rules make the top insight blunt. **If the total due isn't cleared, the interest-free period is lost and interest runs from the transaction date** ([RBI Master Direction](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasterDirections.aspx?id=12300); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/rbi-s-new-guidelines-on-credit-cards-what-it-means-for-cardholders-know-details-here-news-192736)). Joy's billing-cycle data already supports an "interest-free days if you swipe today" ranking per card, a utilisation nudge before the statement date, a revolve warning, and an EMI-vs-revolve calculator that exposes "no-cost EMI" fees. CIBIL does not publish its factor weights, so any score-driver view must say its weights are estimates ([BankBazaar](https://bankbazaar.com/cibil/cibil-score-calculation.html)). For debt payoff, avalanche is cheaper, but **Gal and McShane found that closing individual accounts, which the snowball method encourages, predicted eventually eliminating all debt** ([Kellogg](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/news_articles/2012/snowball-approach.aspx)). Joy should show both methods with the interest cost of choosing snowball.

Expectations for nudges should stay modest. **Nudge-unit trials at scale averaged 1.4 percentage points, against 8.7 in published academic papers** ([NBER](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w27594/w27594.pdf)). Reminders raised savings about 6% in bank field experiments ([J-PAL](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/media/file-research-paper/getting-top-mind-how-reminders-increase-saving)). Due-date reminders, salary-day auto-transfer defaults and fee salience are worth building. "Spending personality" labels are engagement features, not interventions.

## Life planning needs Indian defaults, not imported rules of thumb

Joy's goals and what-if engine needs four upgrades.

**Per-category inflation.** Headline CPI on the new 2024=100 series was 5.54% in September 2026 ([Adda247](https://currentaffairs.adda247.com/indias-retail-inflation-rises-to-5-54-in-september-2026-as-food-prices-surge/)). RBI raised the repo to 5.50% on 7 October 2026 and projects FY27 CPI at 5.2% ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/10/09/rbi-hikes-repo-rate-to-5-50-percent-october-2026/)). Aon projects medical cost inflation at 11.5% for 2026 and 10.1% for 2027 ([Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/medical-costs-in-india-projected-to-rise-10-1pc-in-2027-aon)). Education is conventionally put at 10-12% with no official series ([Basunivesh](https://www.basunivesh.com/cost-of-education-in-india-2025-2040-fees-living-projections/)). Weddings averaged about ₹39.5 lakh in 2025-26, up 8%, with 79% of couples paying from savings ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/loan/big-fat-weddings-cost-couples-their-hard-earned-savings-only-15-per-cent-use-loans-report)). Sensible editable defaults are 6% general, 10% education and medical, and 8% wedding. A line-item wedding budget beats anchoring on a survey skewed toward affluent urban couples.

**Returns shown real and post-tax.** Official rates are PPF 7.1%, SSY 8.2%, NSC 7.7% for Q3 FY27 and EPF 8.25% for FY26 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/story/small-savings-scheme-interest-rates-unchanged-for-q3-fy27-check-ppf-scss-ssy-rates-558892-2026-09-30); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/story/epfo-approves-825-interest-rate-for-2025-26-518810-2026-03-02)). Equity history is contested: Nifty 50 TRI 20-year CAGR is cited at about 12.4% by one source and 11.4% by another, depending on the window ([Bajaj AMC](https://www.bajajamc.com/knowledge-centre/nifty-50-historical-returns); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/mutual-funds/story/can-indian-equities-and-mutual-funds-still-create-long-term-wealth-for-investors-531157-2026-05-13)). A 10-11% equity default is defensibly conservative. The insight that matters for this user is that **a 30%-slab taxpayer earns roughly 4.6-4.9% post-tax on a 6.5-7% FD or RD, which is a negative real return at 6% inflation**, because debt funds bought after April 2023 and deposit interest are both taxed at slab rate ([Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-is-ltcg-tax-rate)). RDs and FDs remain the right home for goals under three years. They are a poor home for long-horizon money.

**Goal mechanics.** For each goal: inflate today's cost to the target year, subtract the future value of money already earmarked, solve for a flat or step-up SIP (annuity-due), and move along a glide path from about 70-80% equity beyond seven years to liquid funds or FDs inside one year. A funding waterfall decides the order when surplus is short: emergency fund, then insurance, high-interest debt, near-term non-negotiables, retirement and the rest. That turns joy's goals list into an allocation plan. The glide-path bands and waterfall are adviser convention, not regulation.

**Retirement.** **Indian safe-withdrawal research (Raju and Saraogi, 2000-2023 data) recommends 3-3.5% at 95% confidence, not the US-derived 4%** ([Business Standard](https://www.pressreader.com/india/business-standard/20240304/282149296266369)). That implies a corpus of roughly 28-33x first-year expenses, and more for early retirement. Inputs come from data joy can import: the EPF passbook, PPF, NPS and the MF holdings from CAS.

Insurance and housing sizing are rules of thumb and should be presented that way. Term cover commonly starts at 10-15x annual income plus loans, rising to 15-20x with children, and can be refined with a human-life-value calculation ([Policybazaar](https://www.policybazaar.com/term-insurance/articles/how-much-term-insurance-do-i-need/)). Health cover erodes at medical-inflation pace, so joy should re-test it every few years. For a house, banks allow fixed obligations of 50-60% of net income, but advisers suggest keeping all EMIs within 35-40% ([Finnovate](https://www.finnovate.in/learn/blog/home-loan-emi-affordability-india)). Joy's "can I afford it" should show the bank's ceiling as a hard limit and the comfort rule as the recommendation.

Tax is where joy can be sharply useful. Budget 2026 left the new-regime slabs unchanged: nil to ₹4 lakh, then 5-30% in ₹4 lakh steps. **The 87A rebate makes taxable income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free, or ₹12.75 lakh of salary after the ₹75,000 standard deduction** ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/tax/story/tax-slabs-fy-2026-27-what-budget-2026-changed-for-individual-taxpayers-and-which-regime-works-best-514044-2026-02-01)). For higher salaries, the old regime generally wins only when deductions exceed about ₹4 lakh ([Basunivesh](https://www.basunivesh.com/new-tax-regime-vs-old-regime-who-wins-in-fy-2026-27/)). A regime picker that computes both regimes from the user's own data and shows the break-even deduction is a direct extension of the existing tax panel. Equity LTCG stays at 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh, with STCG at 20% ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/taxation/union-budget-2026-ltcg-tax-unchanged-at-12-5-stt-on-commodity-futures-hiked)). With CAS cost basis in hand, joy can nudge an annual harvest of gains up to that exemption, separately for each spouse. AIS reconciliation then confirms that FD/RD interest and dividends match what the tax department sees. The Income Tax Act 2025 took effect on 1 April 2026 and renumbers sections ([Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/income-tax-changes-for-fy-2026-27)), so app copy should show old and new section names side by side. The 87A marginal relief, the HRA metro list and the new section numbers were not verified against primary text and need checking before shipping.

## Couples do better with shared goals and visible fairness than merged accounts

The evidence favours "financial togetherness" over any particular account structure. In a randomised six-wave experiment with 230 engaged or newlywed couples, **couples assigned to open a joint account held relationship quality steady while the separate-account and control groups declined** ([Kellogg Insight](https://insight.kellogg.northwestern.edu/article/key-to-happy-marriage-joint-bank-account)). A six-study paper (N = 38,534) found pooling predicts higher satisfaction, mediated by seeing goals as shared, and stated that autonomy itself is not harmful. In British cohort data, 30% of fully separate couples had split a decade later, against 26% of partial poolers and 24% of full poolers ([UCLA Anderson Review](https://anderson-review.ucla.edu/joint-bank-account)). Pooling has a cost: people feel they must justify purchases to their partner ([Notre Dame Mendoza](https://mendoza.nd.edu/?p=7598)). Money fights centre on fairness and responsibility. In India, an advisory-firm survey found 67% of couples argue about money regularly, and 56% of women earned less than their husbands ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/personal-finance/42-men-took-loans-for-alimony-46-women-reduced-work-after-marriage-survey)). A BankBazaar study, cited secondhand, found 38% fear misuse of a joint account ([LoansJagat](https://www.loansjagat.com/news/how-indian-couples-are-redefining-money-management-in-modern-relationships)). Neither Indian source is peer-reviewed.

These findings validate joy's design and point to specific additions. The "ours" layer (date jar, savings jar, joint goals) should be the emotional centre, with private accounts allowed. Per-account visibility of shared, balance-only or hidden follows the Honeydue pattern and respects autonomy. A small private no-questions allowance per partner answers the justification cost, though that idea is untested. Settle-up should show its working: who paid, which split rule, and the running balance. Income-proportional splitting is relevant given Indian income gaps, though no study compares it with 50/50. Family obligations (transfers to parents, each side's wedding contributions) deserve their own tags so they don't muddy couple spending. The market gap is real: YNAB's own guidance is to pair it with Splitwise for settle-up ([YNAB](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/splitwise-and-ynab-a-guide-H1GwOyuCq.md)), and Google Pay's group split is basic ([Google Pay Help](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/11420982?hl=EN)). UPI deep links can settle up without joy ever moving money.

Joint tax planning is a couples feature no app offers. **Each co-owner who is also a co-borrower on a home loan can claim ₹2 lakh of interest and ₹1.5 lakh of principal under the old regime, but a partner on the new regime loses both for a self-occupied home** ([Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/tax-benefits-on-joint-home-loan/); [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/claim-tax-benefits-joint-home-loans)). Optimising each spouse's regime jointly, and using two separate ₹1.25 lakh LTCG exemptions, are concrete household savings. A monthly "money date" can attach to the existing month-close review and date jar, combining rollover, settle-up and goal progress in one ritual.

## Code computes every number; the LLM only labels and narrates

DPDP is unlikely to bite while joy stays personal. **Section 3(c)(i) excludes personal data processed by an individual "for any personal or domestic purpose"** ([dpdpa.com](https://dpdpa.com/dpdpa2023/chapter-1/section3.html)). Hosting joy for other couples would make the operator a Data Fiduciary, with core duties phasing in around May 2027 under the Rules notified in November 2025 ([Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/govt-notifies-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-rollout-in-phases-specified-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries)). Rule 6 names a sensible voluntary baseline anyway: encryption or masking, access control, access logs kept for a year, and backups ([dpdpa.com Rule 6](https://dpdpa.com/dpdparules/rule6.html)). Whether one person running the app for both partners still counts as "domestic" has no authority either way, so explicit in-app consent from the partner is cheap insurance.

Before email ingest brings in statements and CAS, joy should encrypt SQLite at rest. **SQLCipher encrypts each page with AES-256 and authenticates it with HMAC-SHA512, with the key derived via PBKDF2** ([Zetetic](https://www.zetetic.net/sqlcipher/design/)). It only helps if the key lives in a Fly secret injected at boot, never on the volume. Backups should be separately encrypted. An append-only audit log should record partner views and exports. Passkeys or TOTP, HttpOnly SameSite cookies and a strict CSP complete the baseline.

For LLM features, retention and injection are the two risks. **Anthropic's commercial API deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days, but flagged content can be kept longer, and zero retention requires a separate agreement** ([Anthropic Privacy Center](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data)). Reports of 2026 policy changes are contradictory, so check at build time. Narrations are attacker-controlled text: anyone who sends ₹1 writes into your statement. That is the indirect prompt injection OWASP lists as LLM01, which becomes dangerous when combined with excessive agency, LLM06 ([promptfoo](https://promptfoo.dev/docs/red-team/owasp-llm-top-10/)).

The architecture follows from that. Strip VPAs, names, phone numbers and reference IDs before any call. Send normalised merchant names and aggregates. Give the model no write tools. Validate category outputs against an enum. Render output as text, never HTML. For Q&A, **routing questions to a small set of vetted, parameterised query functions is more reliable than free text-to-SQL** ([arXiv 2510.14162](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14162v1)). Joy's Rust functions return exact paise and pre-formatted ₹ strings, the model fills placeholders, and a post-check rejects any number not in the tool results. The safest high-value LLM feature is a monthly narrative written from a deterministic "facts pack", cached per month and attached to the month-close review. A local model via an Ollama-compatible endpoint should be a configurable option, but on a GPU-less Fly VM it will be slow. A sensible split is rules for categorisation and an API model only for occasional narratives over pseudonymised aggregates. Every outbound payload goes in the audit log, and LLM features stay off for the partner until they opt in.

## Conclusion

The research reframes joy's next phase. It already has the planning features that Origin and Monarch charge for. What separates a planning tool people trust from one they abandon is whether the numbers are right, and in India that comes down to plumbing: catching the sub-₹100 UPI payments SMS misses, separating card-bill payments from spend, and seeing SIP holdings at cost basis. Build that pipeline and features that are now estimates (safe-to-spend, net worth, the tax panel) become exact, while new ones such as LTCG harvesting, regime choice and refund deadlines become possible. Joy's position is also unusually defensible. Self-hosting is the one way to get Account-Aggregator-grade coverage (joint accounts, FDs, CAS) without a licence, and a couples product that treats fairness as a visible calculation and shared goals as the centre is something the market has repeatedly failed to sustain.

The largest open risks are empirical, not technical. Bank SMS and email templates, Kotak's export formats and the fine print of RBI's compensation rules must be verified against the user's own inbox and statements. Several planning defaults (glide paths, insurance multiples, house heuristics) are adviser convention rather than evidence, and the app should label them that way. Behavioural nudges move outcomes by single-digit percentages at best. Joy's real lever is automation and defaults, such as salary-day transfers into goals, rather than clever messaging.
